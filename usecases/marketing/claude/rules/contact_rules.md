# Contact sheet rules

The agent only **proposes** changes. A person approves each change before it goes into the sheet.

## Create a new contact when

- a real person or organisation writes to us with a genuine request (institute, member, partner, event, press), and
- they are not in the sheet yet (check the email address and the name).

Also create a contact for unknown senders who make a serious offer we cannot verify yet. Set the status to `unverified`.

Do **not** create contacts for newsletters, spam, phishing, automatic mails, or service offers we ignore.

## Update a contact when

- the sender writes from a new email address (keep the old one in the notes),
- the contact has no email address on file and the sender is clearly the same person,
- we reply: set "last contact" to the date of the email,
- the status changes (e.g. from `Prospect` to `Interested`, or a vendor we declined).

## Fields

name, organisation, role, email, category, language (DE/EN), topics, last contact (YYYY-MM-DD), status, notes.

Status words: `Contact`, `Prospect`, `Interested`, `Active user`, `Active`, `Declined`, `unverified`.

## Notes

- Read the notes before you draft (e.g. "declined before", "asked about data protection") and use them.
- Write short notes: what they asked and when.
