# Simplified travel rules (example for the workshop, NOT the official rules)

**Order of steps**
1. The traveller submits a travel request (Reiseantrag) in the SAP portal (ESS).
2. After approval the traveller gets an email "DRA: MPDL/<Reisenummer>/<Personalnummer>/<Name>" with the travel number (Reisenummer).
3. The traveller tells office management where they are going (email or Mattermost), sometimes by forwarding the DRA email.
4. Office management looks for and books hotel and transport. Bookings only once the trip is approved.
5. Training (Fortbildung): first the paper training request (Fortbildungsantrag), then the SAP travel request if travel is needed.
6. After the trip the traveller files the travel expense claim (Reisekostenabrechnung) in the SAP portal.

**Home base**
- Home base: Munich (MPDL office). All trips start and end there, unless the traveller says otherwise. Use it as the start point for train and flight searches.

**Example limits**
- Train: 2nd class by default; 1st class only for trips over 4 hours with approval.
- Flights: only if the train takes more than 6 hours one way, economy class.
- Hotel: up to 120 EUR per night in Germany (up to 150 EUR in Munich, Berlin, Hamburg, Frankfurt), breakfast included if possible.
- Conference fees: early-bird rate whenever possible.
